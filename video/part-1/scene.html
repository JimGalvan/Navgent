Rescheduling Product Sprint Review leaves the meeting showing in both calendar slots

Noticed this while checking room availability for Tuesday. Product Sprint Review, organized by Jordan Lee in Conference Room A, initially appeared only at 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM on . After moving it to the afternoon, the details were correct but the calendar still showed the meeting in the old slot too.

Result

Product Sprint Review appears only at 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM. Its original 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM slot is available.
Actual
The details show 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM. The calendar shows Product Sprint Review twice: at 10:00 AM and at 1:00 PM. Both remain after a refresh.
